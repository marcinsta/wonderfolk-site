Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Wonderfolk does not collect any personal data.

  • No accounts: the app has no sign-up and no login.
  • No tracking: the app contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs.
  • On-device only: your pantry selections, your night order, and the nights you mark as done are stored only on your device and are never transmitted anywhere.
  • No network content: the 2026 season's content is bundled in the app; the app does not send your information to any server.

If a future version adds features that involve data (for example, an optional account or content downloads), this policy will be updated and the App Store privacy details revised before that version ships.
